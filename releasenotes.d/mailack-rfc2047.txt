xymon-mailack -- decode RFC 2047 encoded-words in a reply's headers
-------------------------------------------------------------------

An acknowledgement sent by replying to an alert mail now has the From and
Subject headers MIME-decoded. A non-ASCII acknowledger name or message text,
which a mail client sends as an RFC 2047 encoded-word (=?utf-8?...?=), is
decoded to its bytes and shows as written on the status page instead of as the
raw =?...?= wrapper. A subject a client encoded in full is decoded before the
alert cookie is matched, so such a reply is acknowledged rather than silently
dropped. The bytes are carried under the charset the pages declare
(HTMLCONTENTTYPE, UTF-8 by default); a reply in another charset is not
converted. The message body is not yet decoded.
