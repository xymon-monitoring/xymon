xymond_channel, xymond_worker -- reach a network worker over IPv6
-----------------------------------------------------------------

A network worker's --listen and the channel that connects to it were IPv4
only. Both now take IPv6 and names, as [addr]:port or name:port. A name that
resolves to IPv4 is still used, so an existing locator setup is unchanged. An
IPv6 --listen takes IPv6 connections only, [::] included.
