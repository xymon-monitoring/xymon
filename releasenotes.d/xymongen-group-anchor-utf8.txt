xymongen -- a group title's anchor keeps its non-ASCII letters
--------------------------------------------------------------

The <A NAME> jump target xymongen makes from a group title reduced every
non-ASCII byte to an underscore, so an accented title like "Réseau"
anchored as "group-R_seau" and a non-Latin one collapsed to nothing. The
anchor now keeps those bytes ("group-Réseau"). HTML metacharacters are
still reduced. A bookmark to an old underscore anchor no longer resolves;
re-save it from the page.
