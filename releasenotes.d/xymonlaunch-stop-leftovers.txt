xymonlaunch -- stopping it ends what its periodic tasks left running
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Stopping xymonlaunch, by whatever started it -- runclient.sh, an init
script, systemd, launchd -- now also ends the vmstat the client leaves
running for its five-minute sample. Before, it stayed for up to five
minutes after the client or the server was stopped, and a quick restart
had two of them running. The sample it was taking is discarded, where it
was reported with the averages since boot in place of the sample.

This holds for anything a task with an INTERVAL or a CRONDATE starts: such
a task runs in a process group of its own, and what is left of the group is
sent a SIGTERM when xymonlaunch stops. If one of your own tasks starts a
command that must keep running after xymonlaunch stops, start that command
with setsid, or move it out of the task. Tasks that stay up, such as
xymond, are stopped as before.
