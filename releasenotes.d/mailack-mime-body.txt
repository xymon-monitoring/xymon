xymon-mailack -- decode the body of an alert reply for the cause
----------------------------------------------------------------

Following the header decoding, the body of a reply is now decoded for the
acknowledgement cause. A base64 or quoted-printable transfer-encoding is
decoded, and for a multipart reply -- what most mail clients send -- the first
text/plain part is used and its HTML alternative ignored. So the explanation an
operator typed in the reply shows on the status page instead of a line of
base64 or a MIME boundary. The message is bounded against a hostile sender
(capped size, part count and nesting depth); charsets other than UTF-8 are
still carried, not converted.
