xymond -- a disk status with every filesystem excluded is clear
---------------------------------------------------------------
A client whose XYMONCLIENT_FS_* settings exclude every mounted filesystem
sends no disk report, and says so. Its "disk" status was yellow, "Expected
strings not found in df output"; it is now clear, "No filesystems to check".
This concerns AIX clients today, and the other Unix clients once they send
the same line.

A DISK rule that requires a filesystem to exist still alerts. To have green
where there is nothing to check, xymond_client takes --clear-color=green.
