xymongen -- the RSS feed declares its charset and stays well-formed
-------------------------------------------------------------------
xymongen's --rss feed wrote a bare "<?xml version='1.0'?>" and escaped only
the item description, so a non-UTF-8 charset was read as UTF-8 and a title, a
link with a query "&", or an invalid byte could make the feed malformed. It
now declares the pages' charset (HTMLCONTENTTYPE) and emits every field as
well-formed XML, as the appfeed and perfdata feeds already do.
