xymonnet -- the sslcert subject and issuer are shown in RFC 2253 form
---------------------------------------------------------------------

The sslcert status shows a certificate's subject and issuer. They were
written in the non-standard "/C=.../O=..." form, with every byte above
7-bit ASCII escaped as "\xHH"; they are now the standard RFC 2253 form
("CN=...,O=...,C=..."), and an accented field is kept as UTF-8.

Anything that reads the subject or issuer out of the sslcert status text
sees the new form; nothing in Xymon itself parses it.
